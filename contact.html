Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI fertilizer recommendation work?

Our AI system analyzes crop type, soil conditions, season, and farm size to provide personalized fertilizer recommendations using advanced algorithms and agricultural data.

Is this system suitable for small-scale farmers?

Yes! Agritech is designed to be accessible for farmers of all scales, from small family farms to large agricultural operations.

Can I contribute to the project?

Absolutely! This is an open-source project. Feel free to reach out for collaboration opportunities or check our GitHub repository.

What technologies are used in this project?
